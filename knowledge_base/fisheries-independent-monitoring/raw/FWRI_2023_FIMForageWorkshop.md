## Methods worth reusing

- Stratified-random design with space (and sometimes habitat) strata, quoted above from slide 10.
- Gear and effort definitions: slide 7 gear sizes and start years, with the note on slide 27 that "Calculations (effort, length extrapolations, splitter expansions) explained" are documented in FIM metadata.
- Standard FIM covariates recorded at each sample: temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, pH, substrate, biota cover, vegetation and relief (slides 15 and 16).
- Cold-event and red-tide response analysis pattern: long-term abundance series with a marked event year, shown for Common Snook (slide 23) and for a fish shown on slide 22 (species not named).
- Slide 35 notes the approach to "investigate the ability of statistical models to predict how many samples would be needed to capture diversity and abundance in a system with various habitat types, or the ability to extrapolate abundance data" (slide 35).
- Slide 32 proposes to "Revisit salinity relationships/functions for various species" (slide 32).
